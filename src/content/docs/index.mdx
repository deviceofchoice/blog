### Modern tech doesn't inspire anymore.

Tech companies have turned innovation into planned obsolescence and phones designed to grab attention.
They push new devices every year, abandoning the old ones, even when they still work perfectly.

### This project was born as an attempt to resist that trend.

To help you find and revive usable, reliable phones that respect your time and last longer than the next marketing cycle.
